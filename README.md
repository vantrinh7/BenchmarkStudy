# BRAG Benchmark Study

This study investigates the cooperative binding of oxygen to hemoglobin, focusing on the application of the Hill and Adair equations to modeling oxygen-hemoglobin interactions. Protein-ligand binding is a foundational concept in biochemistry with critical implications for human physiology. Deepening our understanding of these oxygen binding mechanisms supports the advancement of biomedical applications and therapeutic interventions for respiratory and cardiovascular diseases.

## Difficulty breakdown
This sample task is difficult for multiple reasons. <br>
* **Advanced biochemical concepts and areas of active research**: Topics requiring not just advanced knowledge but also research skills can stump models. <br>
* **Easily confused mathematical notations**: There are multiple versions of the equation used in the task (resulting from extensive studies in the field). The model needs to select the correct version among very closely related and easily confused versions. <br> 
* **Multi-step problem solving**: Solving problems that require multiple steps to reach the conclusion is a way to stump the models. The models may oversimplify the problem, remove necessary steps, and reach a wrong conclusion.  <br>
* **Interpreting and transforming graphs**: This task requires the interpretation of a biphasic graph, which is uncommon and rather difficult to comprehend. Transforming axes and generating a completely new graph to solve the problem adds another layer of difficulty, as models can struggle to come up with graph generation as a step to solving the problem. <br>

## File Description
* **Sample task and solution**: This file contains a sample task and the solution. The task requires comprehending a biphasic oxygen hemoglobin binding curve, transforming linear axes to log axes, applying the correct form of the Hill equation, and calculating the final values of the partial pressures of oxygen at half saturation for hemoglobin and myoglobin. <br>

* **Oxygen-hemoglobin binding data**: The first sheet contains the raw data points of partial pressure of oxygen pO<sub>2</sub> and oxygen saturation Y/(1-Y) for wild-type hemoglobin (Hb), myoglobin (Mb), recombinant hemoglobin (rHb), and hemoglobin treated with inositol hexaphosphate. The second sheet contains a plot of pO<sub>2</sub> versus Y/(1-Y) with each data point labeled with exact x and y coordinates. The third sheet contains plots before and after the transformation of linear axes into logarithmic axes. <br>
  
* **Analysis of responses from current frontier models (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini)**: This file contains the responses from Claude Sonnet 5.5., GPT 6, and Gemini 3.8 Flash when presented with the task and an analysis of where the responses are wrong.
